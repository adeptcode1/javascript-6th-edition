We are specialists in building projects from conceptualization to realization. Developing software is our passion. This started for many of us at an early age. We were the kids that wanted to look under the hood of our favorite toys in the early days of personal computing.

Our Founder

Our founding developer has been learning leading programming languages for the past 25 years. Starting when he was 12, he educated himself in the BASIC programming language on a Commodore 64. This interest continued to grow as equipment continued to evolve.

The knowledge gained from that endeavor was transferred to MS-DOS based systems, developing projects in QBASIC. Four years later, his demonstrated understanding of that language allowed him to bypass it as a prerequisite in high school. He proceeded to enroll in classes for the C++ programming language.

He proceeded gaining expertise in software and hardware. He acquired an Associates Degree in Electronics Engineering Technology. Understanding both the Windows and Linux operating systems, he is well versed in server environments. He is currently working on his B.S. in Information Technology, specializing in Software Development with an emphasis on Web Applications Development.

He is a firm believer in active learning. He furthers his coding skills with each project undertaken. Each project solidifies his processes for the working on the next one.
